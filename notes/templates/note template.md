## Frontmatter options

**title**: The note's name. Quartz uses it as the page title.

**type**: Pick one.
- concept: one idea in my own words
- source: notes on a paper, book, or article
- how-to: steps to accomplish something
- belief: one opinion, written as a full sentence
- project: something I built
- hub: a map linking related notes on a theme
- log: dated entries

**status**: Pick one.
- seedling: rough, partial, may be wrong
- budding: taking shape, mostly coherent
- evergreen: something I would stand behind

**tags**: Optional labels a folder cannot express.
- `[baseball]`
- `[ml, statistics]`
- `[rust, systems]`

**created**: Filled in by the Obsidian Templates plugin.

**Optional extras**: Add only when useful.
- `author`, `year`, `link`: for source notes
- `repo`: for project notes
- `draft: true`: Quartz skips publishing this note
